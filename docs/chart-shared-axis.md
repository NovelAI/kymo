Chart logic written by Kevin (no AI):


After downsampling (or not downsampling), every run's points share exactly the same x buckets. So they all either downsample or don't downsample, and they do so the same way.
Each downsample bucket also emits the smoothed value in the center of the bucket, where the x-position displays at the average of the first and last endpoints (so a bucket with steps 1 and 2 emits at 1.5). If the x-axis or y-axis has multiple values, the range will be displayed.

Buckets are always disjoint, having no overlap. Half-open intervals help here.
Smoothed values will always calculated at the center point of the bucket, even if the run's x-values do not reach there.

Be careful of how chart incremental updating works with this logic, since values can be inserted in existing buckets (typically at the endpoints of a run's existing points).

When hovering, highlight the run's values.

Points are never dropped - each point is represented in the output chart. There is one exception: a point can be overridden by a non-finite value, which is more important.

Downsampling should kick in only when a bucket expects 4 or more points, because a bucket with 2 points (rendering an envelope) is worse than 2 buckets with 1 point.

## Current implementation

- The shared axis mixes raw slots for exact x values with envelope slots for downsampling buckets. Step-axis cells become envelopes at width >= 4 (data-independent; missing points are fine); other axes require four observed distinct x values. Every point, including the global minimum and maximum x values, follows its cell's ordinary raw-or-envelope rule. See `shared_chart`/`plan_axis` in chart.rs.
- Linear grids use anchored power-of-two widths. Log grids use power-of-two subdivisions within each octave; step and timestamp charts use `log(x + 1)` when x = 0 is present (the frontend mirrors the same zero-present rule off the model — uplot_chart `log_shift`). Values outside the log domain use the exceptional-marker channel.
- In unsmoothed envelope slots, `values` is the bucket mean and the min/max envelope is the rendered raw evidence. A run with spread (`min < max`) anywhere in its loaded series gets faint 1px envelope borders. If it has no spread, its coincident edges become one full-strength 1.5px line in the raw block. That line uses the bounds because a bucket containing a non-finite sample can retain finite raw evidence while its `values` entry is NaN. This is per series, not per segment, so a partly collapsed envelope keeps consistent border styling. With smoothing, `values` is drawn full-strength over both faint raw edges, even when they coincide; `raw_values` is limited to smoothed passthrough charts.
- Line series bridge their gaps and draw samples only as segments, so a series whose column holds one sample on the chart (one raw point, or one bucket) would draw nothing; it gets a mark instead (create.js `linePaths`). A line gets a dot in its stroke, faint on the raw scatter. An envelope gets a bar from the bucket's min to its max on its min edge, or a dot when they're within a dot of each other: in the run's color at 1.5px on unsmoothed charts, in the faint edge color and width behind a smoothed curve. Two or more samples draw the ordinary line or band, however close they sit. Like uPlot's dots, a mark outside the x window or wholly outside the y range isn't drawn.
- On step and time charts every smoothed value, at envelope, raw and passthrough slots alike, is the run's smoother evaluated at the slot x over its fetched samples, zoom margins included (`SlotEvaluator` in chart.rs): a Savitzky–Golay fit centered there, or a causal smoother's state after its last sample at or before the slot x, read there without a new sample. Custom x smooths in step order, so it has no window at a slot x: it interpolates its smoothed points there, extrapolating along the nearest segment past a run's points.
  - A step or time chart that downsamples widens each smoother to at least its floor R·w(x) (`Floor`, NovelAI/kymo#16), so a slot is about as smooth as its bucket's mean: over evenly sampled white noise on a linear grid, with samples dense in each bucket and the floor setting the width, its variance is within 5% of the mean's except in a run's first buckets, where a causal smoother has read fewer samples, and its last. A bucket of few samples is smoothed further (an order-0 EMA at time constant 1 over buckets of 4 steps logged every 2 steps: 0.92 of the mean's variance; every 4 steps, 0.76). w is the bucket width on a linear grid, or (x + shift)/s on the log ladder, s its buckets per octave and shift the ladder's (1 when the chart holds x = 0, else 0): it equals the ladder's bucket at each octave's start, stays under twice it and never steps. R is ∫K² of the smoother's kernel there: Savitzky–Golay 5/7 at orders 0–1 and 805/572 at order 2, EMA 1/2, 5/4 and 33/16 at orders 0, 1 and 2; Triangular has no width to widen. The floor depends only on the grid's tier, so appends keep it until a re-tier. A bounded step zoom's margins cover the floor (a Savitzky–Golay window exactly, an EMA to eight time constants at the zoom's first step) and the user's Savitzky–Golay window to 4·window steps (8,000 at the UI's widest), which holds the whole window while the series' median gap is at most 8 steps: a sparser series' zoom edges read a one-sided window, as at a data edge.
  - A floored Savitzky–Golay window always reaches all its bucket's samples. Past a run's samples, where other runs stretch its bucket, and alike before a late or resumed run's first samples, the window holds samples on one side only, and a fit of order 1 or 2 is read beyond them: with the run's last sample at its bucket's start, that slot's spread over white noise is about 32 (order 1) or 21 (order 2) times a slot's usual (2.0 and 1.3 times the raw noise's at 256 samples a bucket), falling to about 2.7 and 2.8 times by the bucket's middle. An EMA or Triangular slot past the run continues its last fitted polynomial, holding its last value at order 0, up to half a bucket past it, many times a short run's own length in a wide bucket: beside a run of a billion steps (buckets of 2^21 at 500 pixels), Triangular's line through 3 steps of 2.300 ± 0.002 reads anywhere within about ±2,500 half a bucket (2^20 steps) on, its parabola within ±4.4 billion.
  - A causal slot whose x precedes all of its run's samples in the bucket (its first bucket, or where it resumes after a gap) has no value: its smoother has read none of them.
- On log-y, a nonpositive sample clips below the plot and gets a border circle, like a clipped envelope minimum; its tooltip and copied row keep the value. Hover measures a below-scale value at its slot's circle, if the slot has one (the top one for a +∞ marker). Every other value is measured where uPlot puts it, circle or not: a smoothed line where it is drawn, an unsmoothed bucket mean (never drawn) inside its band. Known limit: a border circle at a slot with no value would read `NaN`, since that marker kind has no label of its own; no known data produces one, so it isn't handled.
- Raw slots carry plottable points exactly; envelope slots retain their min/max evidence; non-finite samples use the marker channel. An exceptional-x (kind-4) marker sits on the slot holding its anchor sample: the run's first plottable x on a log step/time axis, or on custom x the last plottable x before the gap in step order (a leading gap takes the first). That is the anchor's envelope slot or its exact raw slot, never a neighbor. A run with no plottable x puts its marker on column 0. When no run has a plottable point, the response carries each series' unplottable count over an empty axis, and the panel shows that count instead of "No data".
- The wire format stores occupied column ranges and finite f32 values; the client inflates the dense shared-axis model (`chart_sync::inflate_response`). `banded` carries the chart-wide envelope contract, and chart-level `xr_min`/`xr_max` carry bucket x extents.
- Main-page Settings stores two browser-local hover options, both off by default: highlight runs with the same exact name, and show each run's nearest available point in the loaded data when its hovered column is empty. Nearest compares x values, including endpoints and logged non-finite samples, and picks the earlier x on ties. Borrowed rows show their own x or bucket range. Only samples at the hovered column choose the highlighted run; borrowed rows are readouts. Chart hover dims the other runs and lifts that run only in the hovered chart, so a run change repaints one canvas; synced charts still mark its tooltip row. Sidebar hover dims and lifts in every chart.
- Deltas require a cache-lineage stamp proving the exact rows behind the held response; legacy watermark keys are absent so older servers also decline deltas. See [incremental updates](incremental-updates.md).
- `plan_delta_from_col` composes numeric and marker dependencies after lineage, membership, and exact smoothing-plan checks. Smoothed values follow the slot rule (`SlotSmoother::dirty_from`): a continuing series' slots are dirty from its first new fetched sample's x (a causal smoother) or from that x less the Savitzky–Golay half-width there. An envelope column is dirty by its center, whichever runs fill it (a timestamp-axis fetch-margin sample can change a cell the series has no sample near); a raw or passthrough column only where the series has a sample.
- A kind-4 marker moves only with its anchor's cell, which the numeric proof already covers. An anchor moves only to an earlier sample (a time-axis step logged at an earlier timestamp), and that NEW sample dirties its own column, at or before both marker slots. Its appearance does not change any numeric column: when a continuing run's held rows had no unplottable sample, the planner marks the anchor sample ANCHOR, and a column-0 marker appearing for a run with no plottable x answers in full. Verified negative sentinels retain incremental reuse. Sampled server audits reconstruct the verified held row set; client hashes verify every splice. Y is rounded to wire f32 before hashing so the comparison remains bit-exact.
